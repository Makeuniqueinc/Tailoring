Frequently Asked Questions

Who can join the courses?

Women interested in learning tailoring can join. Beginners can start with the Basic course.

What are the class timings?

Classes are conducted in two batches: 10 AM–12 PM and 2 PM–4 PM.

Which days are classes conducted?

Monday to Friday.

Can I choose my batch?

Yes. Students can choose either the morning or afternoon batch according to their convenience.

Can I join Modeling Blouse directly?

Yes, if you have already learned blouse stitching or have prior knowledge. Otherwise, complete the Blouse course first.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes. A Government certificate will be provided, which students can use to get a free machine from the Government.

Is the Basic course suitable for beginners?

Yes. The Basic course is designed for beginners.
